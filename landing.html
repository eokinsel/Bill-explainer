FAQ

Questions, answered

What kinds of bills can I use?

Medical statements, insurance documents, utility bills, and most other bills you can photograph or copy the text from. Bills are sorted into categories like Medical, Insurance, Utility, and Other.

How does insurance matching work?

You add your plan's coverage details once. When you explain a bill, Bill Explainer uses them to show what your plan should cover for each charge and what you're likely to owe.

Is this legal or financial advice?

No. Bill Explainer is an AI-powered tool that helps you read and question your bills. Always confirm important details with your provider or insurer, and consult a professional for legal or financial decisions.

What do I get for free?

Every account can explain one bill free each year, so you can see exactly how it works on a real bill before deciding on Premium.

Can I cancel Premium any time?

Yes. Premium is $5 a month, and you can manage or cancel your subscription from your account at any time.
